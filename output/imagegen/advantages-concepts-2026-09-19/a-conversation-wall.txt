Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one polished high-fidelity desktop website SECTION design for Zora Studio's Chinese AI practical training course. This is a flat, front-on UI design deliverable, not a photograph, not a monitor mockup. Landscape 1536x1024. Render the complete section within the canvas, no cropped content. The user repeatedly rejected low information density, oversized hero introductions, excessive whitespace, generic FAQ tabs, and six uniform rounded feature cards. The module must remain a conversation: learner questions answered by the course. Make a premium, distinctive, implementable design with strong hierarchy and compact, comfortable information density. All SIX advantages should be readable at once. Generous enough spacing to read, but use roughly 85% of the space for meaningful typography and content. No giant empty opening. No navigation bar, no sidebar, no browser chrome, no photographic people, no stock art, no shiny 3D, no gradients, no fake product screenshots.
Brand invariants: light neutral gray #ECECEB edge-to-edge background, near-black #20201F text, pure white contrast surfaces, sparing coral-red #E85B50 accents and warm-gray #666762 secondary text. Crisp Chinese typography with slim geometric Chinese sans for section heading and compact contemporary Chinese sans for body; optionally a restrained slanted display face for a small number of emphatic phrases. Text must be large enough to read on the 1536-pixel wide design. All body copy readable, no lorem ipsum, no decorative gibberish. Questions and answers clearly paired, and distinguish their voices. Small speech tails and concise red reply marks are permitted. Do not use a standard sidebar FAQ interface or hide benefits in tabs. Use only the factual text below; you may break lines but do not add unverifiable claims, prices, mentor credentials, service durations, guaranteed revenue or badges.
